# RVM25 PET reverse vending machine concept

Concept model dimensions: **760 mm wide × 700 mm deep × 1,850 mm high**. The front face points toward negative Y; the floor is Z = 0. Units are millimetres.

## Use in Autodesk Fusion

1. In Fusion, choose **Insert → Insert Mesh** and select `RVM25_reverse_vending_machine.stl`.
2. Set the units to **millimetres** and place the model at the origin.
3. The STL is a faceted concept mesh. If you need editable Fusion solids, use **Mesh → Modify → Convert Mesh** after insertion. You can also use the `.scad` source to change the cabinet dimensions and export an updated STL from OpenSCAD.

The model shows a PET intake, screen and controls, collection-bin window, service door, status light, vents, header and feet. It is a visual concept, not a manufacturing drawing; internal conveyor, compactor, electrical parts, and structural details are not modeled.
